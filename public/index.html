Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't some videos be downloaded?

Private videos, age-restricted content, live streams, and region-locked or copyright-protected videos are not processed by this service. We only surface formats that are legitimately available for a given video.

Where are temporary files stored?

Files are written to a private, server-side temporary directory during processing and are automatically deleted immediately after your download completes, or shortly after if left unclaimed.

Does the service store my videos?

No. Files exist only for the duration of processing and are removed automatically. We do not keep a library or archive of processed content.

Can I download videos I don't own?

You may only use this tool for videos you own or have explicit authorization to download. You are solely responsible for ensuring you have the rights to any content you process here.
